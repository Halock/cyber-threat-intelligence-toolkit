# Threat Actor Profiling

## Purpose

Threat-actor profiling provides a structured method for organizing publicly available information concerning suspected cyber-threat activity.

## Profiling Areas

- Known aliases
- Targeted sectors
- Geographic focus
- Tactics, Techniques and Procedures (TTPs)
- Malware associations
- Infrastructure
- Campaign activity
- MITRE ATT&CK techniques

## Assessment

Threat-actor information should be evaluated against reliable sources and assigned an appropriate confidence level.

No unsupported attribution should be presented as established fact.
