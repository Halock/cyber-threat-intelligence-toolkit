# Domain Intelligence

## Purpose

Domain intelligence involves examining domain-related information to identify infrastructure, relationships and potential security indicators.

## Analysis Areas

- Domain registration information
- DNS records
- Nameservers
- Mail servers
- Subdomains
- Certificate information
- Hosting relationships
- Historical infrastructure

## Intelligence Value

Domain information can help analysts understand infrastructure relationships and identify indicators requiring further investigation.

All examples used in this repository should be simulated or based on publicly available information.
