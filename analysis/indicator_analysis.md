# Indicator Analysis

## Purpose

Indicator analysis involves examining technical indicators to determine their relevance, context and potential relationship to suspicious activity.

## Indicators

- IP addresses
- Domains
- URLs
- File hashes
- Email addresses
- Malware indicators

## Analytical Process

1. Identify the indicator.
2. Classify the indicator type.
3. Validate its format.
4. Enrich the indicator with available information.
5. Compare related indicators.
6. Assess the available evidence.
7. Record the analytical conclusion.

## Confidence

Assessments should clearly distinguish confirmed information, analytical assessment and unverified information.
